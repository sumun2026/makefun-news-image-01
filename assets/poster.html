New Orleans

ALL 129
RESCUED
ON THE RIVER

A tanker tore into the American Heritage, docked on the Mississippi, and left the paddleboat half sunk. Passengers clung to the railings until rescue boats reached them.

All 129 aboard were accounted for; 32 went to hospital with non-critical injuries. By midnight, no one was missing.

New Orleans is calling it a miracle on the Mississippi.
